FAQ

Is mediation intended to stop a divorce?

No. Mediation respects each party's autonomy.

Can mediation begin if only one person is interested?

An initial assessment can begin, but substantive mediation requires voluntary participation by both parties.

Is mediation suitable where there has been family violence?

Not always. Safety and power balance must be assessed first.

Can international couples use mediation?

Potentially, yes. Cross-border matters require consideration of multiple legal systems, cultures, travel and enforceability.

Can the mediator represent me in court afterwards?

Generally, no. To preserve neutrality and avoid role conflicts, once the Center or mediator has acted as a neutral family mediator, the mediator will generally not act for either party as legal counsel or litigation representative in the same or a substantially related dispute. Parties requiring individual representation should obtain independent legal counsel.
